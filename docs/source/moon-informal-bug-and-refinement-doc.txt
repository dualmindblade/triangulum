Other generation features:
Crater ray systems currently resemble snakes. There is color banding from one end of the ray to the other (the ray's main color should be consistent), and rays appear to warp/bend back and forth (rays should be straight). I did mention that rays should be "rougher," but this should be implemented through smaller noise resembling the graininess caused by irregular particle ejection, not large scale warping of the entire band. The position of the rays should not change or warp, rather there should be texture to break up the straight, discrete rays, just like what we see in real life. Also, there are still large gaps between the longer rays at the rim/halo of the impact. These spots should probably be filled with more rays, and the overall ray distribution should be more even radially, but not necessarily in length.
