## One thing I want your eyes on — ANSWERED 2026-07-26, and the answer matters

The STUCK gate fires on `progress > 0.80` AND (`residual > 3.0` OR
`changed_frac > 0.04`). Post-fix B-16 sits at progress **0.57–0.64**, and that
number moved 0.07 between two runs on the same machine. The margin to 0.80 is
real but not large, and build timing varies more across machines than across
runs. Both conditions must trip together, so a false flag also needs residual
above 3.0 (currently 1.96–2.19) — I judge it safe, but it is the one number in
this tool I would want re-checked the first time the reel runs on a different
machine or after a scheduler change.

**Shift-2 coordinator, 2026-07-26 — re-checked, and the concern was pointed at
the right number from the wrong side.** The tail-trend rebuild replaced
`progress` with `tail_ratio`, so the 0.80 margin is moot. But the *residual*
half turned out to be the fragile one, exactly as feared: on today's warm run
of the same specimen, `residual_final` read **2.322 — BELOW the 3.0 trigger**.
