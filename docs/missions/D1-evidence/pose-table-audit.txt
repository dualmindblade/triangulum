Per-table pose audit - D-1, 2026-07-26
======================================================================
Instrument: world_reel.py's premise footprint (a uniform 32x18 SCREEN
grid cast through each pose's own camera onto the local ground datum,
one `probe` per cell). Every fraction below is a fraction of the frame.
Raw output: pose-table-audit-raw.txt.

Two caveats stated up front, because they change how to read this:
  - the model ignores relief and occlusion, so it is OPTIMISTIC: it can
    pass a pose whose class is hidden behind a ridge. It cannot pass one
    whose class is absent.
  - `probe` returns no koppen today, so "is this really savanna/jungle/
    taiga/ecotone" is NOT checkable by anything here. Where a verdict
    below says "sound", it means "contains what the probe can see", not
    "the biome is right".

sky_reel.py POSES (5)                     VERDICT: SOUND - AND AHEAD OF US
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The class here is the SKY, so a ground probe says nothing about it, and
the footprint model is the wrong instrument by construction. Two checks
that ARE valid:

1. sky_reel ALREADY ASSERTS ITS OWN PREMISE, and has all along. Its
   `lint()` is a presence check per pose - sun disc >= 80 px above 250,
   setting sun >= 30 px above 240, moon disc >= 60 px above 120, stars
   >= 40 points. That is exactly the invariant this mission was asked to
   invent for the world reel, done in pixel space at lint time. It is
   the only reel that had it. Worth saying out loud, since the handoff's
   suspicion was that the other tables were "probably as rotten".
2. The sun geometry is self-consistent, checked by hand:
     sun_zenith    camera (10.0, 0.0)   sun (10.0, 0.0)    -> 0 deg, overhead
     sunset_west   camera (10.0, 91.0)  sun (10.0, 0.0)    -> 91 deg, at/just under the horizon
     dusk_gradient camera (10.0, 95.0)  sun (10.0, 0.0)    -> 95 deg, civil dusk
     moon_zenith   camera (-9.5, 176.9) sun (10.0, 0.0)    -> ~177 deg, deep night
     night_stars   same camera, look 120/35               -> deep night
   Every pose is in the lighting regime its name claims.
Ground under the cameras, for the record: sun_zenith / moon_zenith /
night_stars look UP (pitch +75, +75, +35) so the frame is 100% sky;
sunset_west and dusk_gradient sit over ocean at h -6 m.

SUMMARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The handoff's suspicion ("the other reels' pose tables are probably as
rotten as the world reel's") is PARTLY right and mostly wrong.
  - 1 genuinely dead pose outside the world reel: `mountain_mid`, and it
    is dead in TWO tables (perf_reel and convergence_reel) because they
    share the coordinates.
  - 1 implicit-orientation pose: perf_reel's `ascent_sweep`.
  - 1 misnamed but functional pose: perf_reel's `ocean_storm` (a lake).
  - 1 site with a wrong comment and an unsettled question: the ladder's
    `snowline`.
  - sky_reel is not merely sound, it already had the premise check this
    mission was asked to invent.
  - convergence_reel's `b16_ice_hold` - the one the mission said to flag
    rather than touch - is sound.
