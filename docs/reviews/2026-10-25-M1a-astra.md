1. **Face-edge neighbour sampling is wrong.** [game/src/grid.rs:173](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/grid.rs:173)  
   The extra `pc.signum()`/`qc.signum()` reverses extrapolation at negative edges. With default N, `dir(0, -0.5, N/2)` returns face 0 at approximately `i=0.49994`, sampling back inside itself. The subsequent snap also ignores LOD spacing: an L9 halo at `(N+256, N−256)` maps to neighbour coordinates approximately `(255,130561)`, which are not L9 centres. This corrupts border occupancy and would misdirect collision/water neighbours. Replace this with explicit integer face-edge transforms at the requested voxel spacing, including a defined corner policy. **Confidence: certain.**

2. **The default coarsest LOD loses every ocean.** [game/src/worldgen/mod.rs:311](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/worldgen/mod.rs:311), [411](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/worldgen/mod.rs:411)  
   Materials are selected at voxel centres. At L9, the top below-sea voxel’s centre is −256 m. The default bake’s deepest ocean is approximately −157 m; even allowing relief, its floor remains above that centre. Consequently this voxel becomes rock, and the next becomes air: no water survives at L9. Sea-level alignment alone does not preserve oceans. Coarsening must preserve water coverage and surface height, potentially through separate terrain/water surface summaries. **Confidence: certain.**

3. **GPU allocation failure becomes a permanent invisible chunk.** [game/src/stream.rs:239](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/stream.rs:239)  
   Failed `add_mesh` returns `None`, but the chunk is inserted into `loaded` just like a successfully generated empty chunk. It stops being requested, can satisfy `settled()`, and can cause its fallback to be freed. Exhausting or fragmenting the arena during refinement therefore leaves holes even after space becomes available. Distinguish empty geometry from pending/failed upload; preserve coverage and retry after eviction. **Confidence: certain.**

4. **The enclosed-chunk shortcut changes authoritative block contents.** [game/src/worldgen/mod.rs:367](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/worldgen/mod.rs:367)  
   Entirely buried chunks become uniform stone, discarding strata and bottom-layer bedrock. A bottom L0 chunk beneath the hills therefore contains different blocks from the normal per-block generator. Adding any marker anywhere disables this shortcut and changes those unrelated buried chunks back to their actual materials. Excavation and saves would expose this inconsistency. Keep visibility shortcuts in meshing, and produce correct canonical contents—or retain a lazy procedural representation with correct block queries. **Confidence: certain.**

5. **Valid planet sizes leave unvisited strips.** [game/src/grid.rs:135](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/grid.rs:135)  
   `chunks_per_edge` floors the division, although `N = k·H` does not guarantee divisibility by a whole coarse chunk. For `H=2048`, sea=512, `N=33·H=67584`, L9 creates four chunks per edge, covering only 65,536 columns. The remaining 2,048-column strips never enter traversal. Smaller planets also produce chunks extending beyond a face. Support partial boundary chunks with ceiling division and clipping, or choose a hierarchy that exactly covers every permitted N. **Confidence: certain.**

6. **A one-block sea-level change can effectively hang startup.** [game/src/grid.rs:110](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/grid.rs:110)  
   Changing the default sea level from 512 to the legal value 513 makes `max_lod=0`. [stream.rs:209](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/stream.rs:209) then iterates approximately **6.44 billion root chunks per update**. This makes the independently configurable sea-level setting a severe performance trap. Decouple hierarchy depth from sea-level divisibility; represent partial water/coarse radial cells explicitly. Reject unsupported configurations clearly until that exists. **Confidence: certain.**

7. **Terrain below the world bottom does not produce the advertised bedrock floor.** [game/src/worldgen/mod.rs:311](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/worldgen/mod.rs:311)  
   The air/water return precedes `k_bottom`. With vertical scale 1/8 and sea=1024, the approximately −1377 m trench lies below the −1024 m world bottom, so layer zero becomes water—or air with water hidden. The early-outs and terrain-band culling can also bypass clipped boundary surfaces. Enforce world-boundary materials before terrain classification/early-outs, and include clipped surfaces in bounds. **Confidence: certain.**

8. **The horizon occluder can hide underwater terrain and future tunnels.** [game/src/stream.rs:91](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/stream.rs:91)  
   Its radius derives from minimum *land* elevation, excluding deeper ocean floors. At scale 1/8, the shipped bake gives an occluder around −1167 m while the trench reaches approximately −1377 m. With H=4096/sea=1536, nearby fine chunks around that floor can be rejected even when visible underwater. Excavation creates the same problem below the assumed solid shell. Base occlusion on a guaranteed opaque, unexcavatable inner core, not the generated land surface. **Confidence: certain.**

9. **Chunk bounding spheres are too small near cube corners.** [game/src/stream.rs:151](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/stream.rs:151), [mesh.rs:142](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/mesh.rs:142)  
   `half·√3·1.05` assumes nearly orthogonal metre-sized axes. For default face 0, L0, `cx=cy=4095, cz=16`, the declared radius is **30.10 m**, but a chunk corner is **33.81 m** from its centre. Geometry near that corner can disappear during frustum/horizon culling. Compute shared conservative bounds from the actual curvature-grid vertices at both radial extremes; the rendered interpolation lies within their convex hull. **Confidence: certain.**

10. **Height bounds have two independent underestimates.** [game/src/worldgen/mod.rs:240](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/worldgen/mod.rs:240), [worldgen/bake.rs:313](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/worldgen/bake.rs:313)  
    Only two opposite warped corners define the bake rectangle. For L9 footprint `(cx=0,cy=5)`, the query covers bake rows through 767, while a generated column samples around row 770. A peak in that omitted strip can be culled. Separately, the 1/8 Catmull–Rom overshoot allowance is insufficient for the separable bicubic interpolant: a 0/1 tap pattern can reach **1.28125**, exceeding the claimed 1.125 maximum. Bound the entire warped footprint and use the full two-dimensional interpolation bound. **Confidence: certain.**

11. **Water has geometric cracks between LODs.** [game/src/render/shader.wgsl:97](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/render/shader.wgsl:97), [worldgen/mod.rs:384](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/worldgen/mod.rs:384)  
    Adjacent LODs use different piecewise-linear approximations of the same spherical edge. At face 0 near its centre, an L7/L6 sea boundary differs by approximately **1.45 m**; L9/L8 differs by **23.21 m** where those LODs contain water. Lowering halo terrain does not lower the sea surface, so terrain skirts supply no water transition. Stitch shared boundaries or constrain fine boundary vertices to a common transition representation. This is additional to the already documented vertical LOD gap. **Confidence: certain.**

12. **Fallback selection draws overlapping LODs and can discard available coverage.** [game/src/stream.rs:262](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/stream.rs:262), [291](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/stream.rs:291)  
    When one child finishes while a sibling remains pending, both the child and its entire fallback ancestor are drawn. Water blends twice and terrain can intersect or flicker. Conversely, a jump to a much coarser LOD searches descendants only two levels down, then frees deeper usable descendants. Maintain a persistent, nonoverlapping coverage tree and switch complete covers atomically. This also gives incremental traversal a stronger foundation than rebuilding independent sets each frame. **Confidence: certain.**

13. **Replacing chunk Arcs is insufficient ownership for M1b/M2.** [game/src/stream.rs:252](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/stream.rs:252), [326](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/stream.rs:326)  
    The renderer’s streamer owns the only chunk store, unconditionally publishes worker results, and evicts contents according to render demand. If edits merely replace Arcs, a late generation result can overwrite them; walking away from a changed lake can discard its state. Establish authoritative world/body storage independent of render residency. Give jobs revisioned snapshots, reject stale results, invalidate affected bounds/halos, and keep simulation commits ordered by tick rather than job arrival. Scope keys to their planet/body now for the moon and M4 grids. **Confidence: certain as a design trap.**

14. **Save compatibility has no durable generator identity.** [game/src/grid.rs:60](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/grid.rs:60)  
    Configuration records a bake path but no generator version, bake content identity, or projection/table version. Replacing that bake or changing generation code while retaining the same seed changes untouched chunks around saved edits. Before M1b saves ship, persist and validate a complete world-generation manifest and retain compatible generation/assets. The existing same-machine/thread-count tests do not establish cross-machine byte identity; add portable golden chunk fixtures before relying on deterministic replay across targets. **Confidence: certain as a design trap.**

15. **Exploration retains unbounded cache and timing history.** [game/src/stream.rs:128](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/stream.rs:128), [229](C:/code/triangulum/game/src/stream.rs:229)  
    Every visited footprint remains in `range_cache`, and every completed job appends a timing sample, including during normal play. Memory therefore grows with travel and regeneration rather than resident content. The geometry cache also clears wholesale at its limit, creating a future recomputation spike. Bound caches with incremental eviction and restrict detailed timing history to benchmarks or bounded sampling. **Confidence: certain.**
