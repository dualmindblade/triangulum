# Inbox: proposals from X-1 (B-19 diagnosis)

Source: `docs/missions/X1-b19-findings.md` `## Feedback`. Only Austin commits
CLAUDE.md / design-doc changes; this is the proposal, not the change.

## 1. `TRANSITIONS.md` should name the three constants that decide the answer

Root `CLAUDE.md` routes "the mesh↔voxel boundary, LOD morph, popping" to
`TRANSITIONS.md`. That document does not contain the words `MAX_LEVEL`,
`ERR_TARGET`, or "vertex spacing" — yet those three numbers, plus the voxel
patch radius, completely determine what any mesh-drawn boundary can look like.
B-19's whole diagnosis is a consequence of them. Suggested addition to
`TRANSITIONS.md` (wording is a starting point, not a demand):

> **The resolution ladder, in numbers.** Voxel columns are 1.35 m. Mesh
> vertex spacing is `(π/2·R)/(2^level·32)`; `terrain.rs MAX_LEVEL = 14` caps
> it at **25.95 m**, and `renderer.rs ERR_TARGET = 0.35` chooses the level
> from camera→tile distance measured **to the sea-level sphere** (so high
> ground gets coarser mesh at the same height above ground). The voxel patch
> is a disc of `(200 + (2.5 − alt_km)·120)·patch_scale` metres that *shrinks*
> with altitude and vanishes at `VOXEL_MAX_ALT_KM = 2.5`. The per-pixel
> categorical reconstruction begins at `BIOME_RANGE_BLEND_START_KM = 4.0` km
> and the coastline curve restoration at `COAST_RANGE_CURVE_START_ALT_KM =
> 12.0` km. **Between ~0.5 km and ~4 km from the camera, neither the voxel
> field nor the per-pixel path owns a boundary — the mesh vertex lattice
> does.** Any boundary work in that annulus is a vertex-lattice problem.

Rationale: three separate investigations have now landed on the same lattice
(`a65591f`'s "~100 m sawtooth tongues", `d1237cb`'s "a 15 m river cannot
exist in a 103 m lattice", and B-19). Each re-derived it from source.

## 2. `viewer/CLAUDE.md`: say that `TRI_LOD_RING_DEBUG` is *decodable*

Current text: "`TRI_LOD_RING_DEBUG` (false-color LOD levels)". It is more than
that — the palette is exactly invertible
(`0.20 + 0.75·fract(vec3(0.37,0.61,0.83)·band)` with `band = level+1` for
tiles and `0.5` for voxel chunks), so a frame is a **per-pixel map of which
renderer and which LOD level drew each pixel**. It matched 100 % of pixels on
every frame X-1 threw at it. Suggested one-liner:

> `TRI_LOD_RING_DEBUG` — false-colour LOD levels, and the palette is
> invertible: `0.20 + 0.75·fract(vec3(0.37,0.61,0.83)·band)`, `band = level+1`
> for mesh tiles, `0.5` for voxel chunks. Decoding it gives a per-pixel
> renderer/level map (`scripts/x1_lod_owner.py`).

That one sentence is the difference between "a pretty debug view" and "an
instrument". It is also the cheapest way to answer the question
`viewer/CLAUDE.md` warns has fooled two different models ("is this mesh or
voxels?") — better than the magenta diagnostic, because it also gives the
level.

## 3. A rule for diagnosis missions and experimental builds

X-1 needed to build three throwaway binaries from a one-constant edit to
`terrain.rs` (an unowned file) to turn "consistent with the vertex lattice"
into a controlled intervention. It built, measured, reverted, and verified
`git status` clean before committing — but nothing in `docs/PROCESS.md` says
whether that is allowed. It is a genuinely useful permission and it should be
written down rather than inferred each time. Proposed wording for
`docs/PROCESS.md`:

> **Experimental builds.** A diagnosis mission may build throwaway binaries
> from uncommitted edits to files no other mission owns, provided the edits
> are reverted and `git status` is clean before any commit, and the evidence
> packet says exactly which constants were changed. A controlled intervention
> is worth far more than a code-reading, and this is how you get one without
> shipping a fix.

## Filed as findings, not proposals (already in `BUGS.md` / the mission doc)

- `select_tiles` measures LOD distance to the **sea-level** sphere, so the
  same height above ground gets ~2 LOD levels less detail over a 2.4 km
  plateau than over the coast. Probably unintentional; invisible until you go
  looking. → B-23.
- `--patch 2` appears unusable in the harness: a settled `shot` at 300 m AGL
  ran >8 minutes at 2 GB RSS without producing a frame (`--patch 1` at the
  same pose: ~1.3 s). → B-24.
- `altitude_ladder.py`'s `run_play` hardcodes its argument list, so no flag or
  env var can be varied through it; X-1 had to re-implement the
  probe/pose/render pipeline to sweep one flag. A `--play-args` passthrough
  turns the ladder from a survey into an A/B harness.
- The staircase lint's thresholds are calibrated for `MAX_LEVEL = 14`: at
  `MAX_LEVEL = 12` the artefact is 4x worse and the lint fires **zero** times.
  Anyone changing the LOD cap must re-calibrate it or it will report the
  regression as clean.
